2026.05.14.
Find a pair of chain complexes \(C_*\) and \(D_*\) such that the tensor product chain complex \(C_*\otimes D_*\) does not satisfy the K\"unneth formula, i.e., there is some \(n\) such that \[H_n(C_*\otimes D_*)\not\cong\bigoplus_{p+q=n}H_p(C_*)\otimes H_q(D_*)\oplus\bigoplus_{p+q=n-1}\text{Tor}\left(H_p(C_*),H_q(D_*)\right).\]
Solution.
Let \(C_*\) be as follows \[\cdots\to 0\to C_1\xrightarrow{\cdot 2} C_0\to 0\to\cdots,\] where \(C_1=\mathbb{Z},\;C_0=\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z},\;\partial_1:C_1\to C_0\) defined as \(n\mapsto [2n]\), and other \(C_k=0\) for \(k\ne 0,1\). And define \(D_*=C_*\). Then we have \[H_0(C_*)=H_0(D_*)=\frac{\ker\partial_0}{\operatorname{im}\partial_1}=\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z},\quad H_1(C_*)=H_1(D_*)=\frac{\ker\partial_1}{\operatorname{im}\partial_2}=\mathbb{Z},\] and \((C_*\otimes D_*)_0=C_0\otimes D_0=\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), \((C_*\otimes D_*)_1=(C_0\otimes D_1)\oplus(C_1\otimes D_0)=\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), we find that \(\partial^\otimes_1\) from \((C_*\otimes D_*)_1\) to \((C_*\otimes D_*)_0\) is defined as, just induced from \(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\) to \(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), is \(\partial^\otimes_1(c,d)=[2(c+d)]=0\), note that \[\ker\partial_1^\otimes=(C_*\otimes D_*)_1\cong\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z},\quad H_1(C_*\otimes D_*)=\frac{\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}}{\operatorname{im}\partial^\otimes_2},\] but to check K\"unneth formula, we have \[\begin{aligned} &\bigoplus_{p+q=1}H_p(C_*)\otimes H_q(D_*)\oplus\bigoplus_{p+q=0}\text{Tor}\left(H_p(C_*),H_q(D_*)\right)\\ =&\left(H_0(C_*)\otimes H_1(D_*)\right)\oplus\left(H_1(C_*)\otimes H_0(D_*)\right)\oplus \text{Tor}\left(H_0(C_*),H_0(D_*)\right)\\ =&\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\text{Tor}\left(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\right)\cong\ker\left(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{\cdot 2}\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\right)\\ =&\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\not\cong\left(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\right)/\operatorname{im}\partial^\otimes_2.\quad\square \end{aligned}\]
